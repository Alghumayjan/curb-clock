# Curb Clock

Live street-parking guide for Manhattan from 60th to 80th St, river to river, centered on the Upper East Side between 5th and 1st Ave.

**Open it on your phone:** https://alghumayjan.github.io/curb-clock/

- **Map:** where you can legally park by day and hour, read from NYC DOT's parking-sign records. Green is free, blue is metered, dashed yellow means you'd have to move before your stay ends, thin red is no parking.
- **Drive mode:** tap **Guide me to free parking**, then **Use my GPS**. It follows your location, tells you whether the block you're on has free curb on a side you can pull into, and speaks the turns to the nearest free block along the one-way streets. **No spot here** moves you on to the next free block.

Data: NYC Open Data — Parking Regulation Locations and Signs (updated Nov 11, 2026), street centerlines, fire hydrants, and ParkNYC meter rates. Holiday suspensions are loaded through Jan 6, 2027.

Zone edges come from the city's distance-from-corner measurements and can be off by a car length. Driveways and temporary construction signs aren't in the data, and GPS between tall buildings can lag a block. Always read the posted signs, set the app up before you drive, and keep your eyes on the road.
