        Where this comes from. Every rule on the map is read from NYC DOT's parking-sign records (Parking Regulation Locations and Signs, NYC Open Data, updated Nov 11, 2026): the signs on every block face from 60th to 80th St, river to river. Hydrants from NYC DEP; meter rates from ParkNYC; holiday suspensions from NYC DOT's 2026 calendar.

        Sign positions come from the city's distance-from-corner measurements, so zone edges can be off by a car length. Driveways and temporary construction permits aren't in the data. The odds are a rough estimate: NYC doesn't publish live parking occupancy. Always read the signs at the curb.
